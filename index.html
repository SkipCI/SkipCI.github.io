Short videos, made and posted by one pipeline.

SkipCI is a small studio that turns a topic into a finished short video — script, voice, motion design, cover, caption — and then publishes it to the channels its owner has connected.

What it does

Composes

One topic becomes a script, a voice track and an animated piece rendered to video.

Packages

Cover image, title, description and post text are produced with the video, not after it.

Publishes

The finished run is uploaded to the connected accounts — YouTube and Telegram — in one step.

Who it is for

SkipCI is operated privately by its author for their own channels. It is not offered as a public service, and it does not host content on behalf of third parties.

Connected accounts

To publish, SkipCI asks the operator to sign in with Google and grant access to their own YouTube channel. That access is used for one purpose: uploading and managing the videos SkipCI itself produced. Details are in the privacy policy.
